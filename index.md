---
title: Privacy Policy – Race Ready Swim
---

_Last updated: 2026-11-09_

Race Ready Swim does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- Your workouts, meets, profile and custom sets are stored only on your device.
- There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- Calendar export creates a file that you choose where to share; the app does not access your calendar.
- Deleting the app, or using **Context → Reset all data**, removes your data.

**Not medical advice.** The app provides general training guidance only. Consult a coach or doctor before changing your training.
